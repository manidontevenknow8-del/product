## Tips

- Respond within 2-3 hours of query publication
- Always include a quotable soundbite
- Reference specific data/numbers from PetClues where possible
- Keep total response under 400 words
- Match the angle the reporter is looking for
- Don't pitch PetClues directly — let expertise speak for itself
