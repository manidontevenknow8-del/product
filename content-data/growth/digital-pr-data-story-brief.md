# Digital PR Data Story Brief

Template for pitching data-driven stories to journalists, bloggers, and
publications covering pets, health, or technology.

---

## Story Brief Template

### Working Title
[Compelling data-driven headline — think: "X% of Pet Owners Don't Know Their Dog's Vaccination Status"]

### Hook / Newsworthiness
[Why should a journalist care right now? Tie to season, trend, news event, or counterintuitive finding.]

### Key Data Points
1. [Statistic or finding #1 — most surprising/shareable]
2. [Statistic or finding #2 — supports the narrative]
3. [Statistic or finding #3 — adds depth/credibility]

### Data Source
- Source: PetClues platform aggregate data / user survey / breed health database
- Sample size: [N]
- Methodology: [Brief — e.g., "Analysis of X health records across Y breeds"]
- Time period: [Date range]

### Visualizations Available
- [ ] Infographic (ready / will create)
- [ ] Interactive chart
- [ ] Map/geographic breakdown
- [ ] Before/after comparison
- [ ] Breed comparison table

### Target Publications

| Tier | Publication | Angle | Contact |
|------|-------------|-------|---------|
| 1 | [Top-tier pet/health pub] | [Specific angle for them] | [Name/email] |
| 2 | [Mid-tier blog/outlet] | [Their angle] | [Name/email] |
| 3 | [Niche pet community] | [Their angle] | [Name/email] |

### Pitch Email Skeleton

Subject: Data: [Compelling stat] — [Topic] [Exclusivity offer if applicable]

Hi [Name],

I noticed your recent piece on [related article]. We just analyzed [data source] and found something your readers might find interesting:

**[Key finding in one sentence.]**

A few more highlights from the data:
- [Stat 2]
- [Stat 3]

I have [infographic / full dataset / expert commentary] available if you'd like to cover this. Happy to provide an exclusive if timing works.

[Your Name]
PetClues | petclues.com

### Exclusivity Strategy
- Offer 24-48h exclusive to Tier 1 publications
- If declined, move to Tier 2 with "first to cover" framing
- After 72h, publish on own blog and pitch as "recently published data"

## Story Ideas Bank

### Seasonal Angles
- **January:** "New Year pet health resolutions" data
- **Spring:** Vaccination compliance rates before summer travel
- **Summer:** Heat-related emergency searches by breed
- **Fall:** Back-to-routine pet health check-up patterns
- **December:** Holiday pet hazard awareness data

### Evergreen Angles
- Most common health issues by breed (our guide data)
- Vaccination compliance gaps by pet type
- Digital vs. paper health record adoption
- Emergency preparedness among pet owners
- Breed-specific health awareness scores

### Trend-Jacking Opportunities
- New pet adoption surges (post any viral pet content)
- Veterinary cost inflation data
- Pet insurance adoption trends
- AI in pet health (our positioning)

## Production Checklist

- [ ] Data collected and verified
- [ ] Key findings summarized (3 bullet points max)
- [ ] Visual assets created
- [ ] Pitch email drafted
- [ ] Target list built (10-15 contacts minimum)
- [ ] Exclusivity timeline set
- [ ] Follow-up sequence planned (Day 3, Day 7)
- [ ] Own blog post drafted (publish after exclusivity window)
