# Vet Clinic Partnership Email — Resource Page Link

Use for outreach to veterinary clinics, animal hospitals, and pet care facilities
that maintain resource pages or client education sections on their websites.

---

## Subject Lines (A/B test)

- A: "Free pet health resource for your clients — PetClues"
- B: "Digital pet health tools your clients would love"
- C: "Partnering with local vet clinics on pet health education"

## Email Template

Subject: [Choose from above]

Hi [Clinic Name / Contact Name],

I came across [clinic name]'s resource page [or: client education section] and wanted to reach out about a potential fit.

I'm [Your Name] from PetClues — we've built a free digital pet health platform that helps pet owners:

- Track vaccination schedules and health records digitally
- Access breed-specific health guides (we cover 200+ breeds)
- Store emergency vet contacts and protocols in one place
- Maintain digital pet passports for travel

**Why this might be useful for your clients:**

Many of your clients likely struggle to keep paper records organized or remember vaccination due dates. PetClues gives them a free digital system to stay on top of their pet's health — which means more engaged clients who show up for preventive visits.

**What I'm asking:**

Would you consider adding PetClues (petclues.com) to your client resources page? We'd be happy to:
- Create a co-branded landing page for your clinic's clients
- Provide educational content for your newsletter
- Feature your clinic as a recommended provider in our directory

Happy to jump on a quick call or just exchange a few emails — whatever works best for your team.

Best,
[Your Name]
Founder, PetClues
[Email]
petclues.com

---

## Follow-up (7 days later)

Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [Name],

Just floating this back to the top of your inbox — totally understand clinic schedules are busy!

If a resource page link isn't the right fit, we're also open to:
- Guest posting a pet health article for your blog
- Providing printable vaccination schedule cards with your branding
- Collaborating on social media pet health tips

Either way, keep up the great work caring for pets in [City]!

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Targeting Criteria

- Clinics with an existing "Resources" or "Links" page
- Clinics that maintain a blog or newsletter
- Multi-vet practices (higher likelihood of marketing staff)
- Clinics in metro areas with tech-savvy clientele
- AAHA-accredited hospitals (quality signal)
